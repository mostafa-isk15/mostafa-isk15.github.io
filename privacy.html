Privacy policy

Policy version 1. This policy describes the private, owner-operated Mimu Calendar integration. Account connections and monitoring features are enabled only after authorization and configuration; this website does not itself access mail or Calendar data.

Who operates the integration

Mimu Calendar is maintained by Mostafa Iskandar for the owner's use. It does not provide public user registration or sell access to connected accounts.

Information accessed and why

  • Email: an authorized read-only mail connection can retrieve message headers, sender and recipient information, dates, subjects, message text, and relevant thread content to answer requests, summarize messages, and identify potential deadlines or travel details. Attachments are accessed only as needed for an authorized task.
  • Calendar: an authorized Calendar connection can retrieve event details and create, change, or delete events when the owner requests or approves that action.
  • Connection information: OAuth credentials and account identifiers support authenticated access. Processing state, such as previously checked message identifiers, may be stored to avoid duplicate notifications.

The Gmail connection uses gmail.readonly; this grant does not permit sending or changing mail. The Google Calendar connection uses calendar.events, which permits event reading and editing. A separate mailbox or provider requires separate authorization. Organizational restrictions must be respected.

Online processing and sharing

The assistant is not entirely offline. Email excerpts, relevant thread content, Calendar details, and user requests may be transmitted to the owner's configured AI provider for the requested analysis. Requested summaries and alerts may be transmitted through Telegram. The owner must authorize these uses and choose configurations permitted by the account provider and any applicable workplace policy.

Only AI processing configurations that do not use connected Google user data to train general-purpose AI models may be used with this integration. Google user data must not be sold, used for advertising, or transferred for unrelated purposes.

Mimu Calendar's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage, security, and retention

Credentials are stored in the owner's local assistant environment and must not be published in this website or its public source repository. Access must be limited to the owner and authorized local processes.

Message excerpts, summaries, Calendar details, and processing results may remain in local assistant history, logs, or task files. Information sent to the configured AI or messaging providers may also remain in those services' history or storage, subject to their settings and policies. This policy does not promise automatic deletion from those services or indefinite secure storage.

The owner controls local retention and the connected services' available history and deletion settings. Only information needed for the authorized task should be processed or retained.

Revoking access and deleting data

Google permissions can be reviewed and revoked in Google Account third-party connections. Other provider permissions must be revoked through their account controls or the institution's administrator.

Revocation prevents further authorized API access but does not automatically erase information already stored in local assistant history or external service history. Those copies must be deleted separately using the relevant service and local data controls.

Public website

This static site contains no mailbox contents, private Calendar entries, access tokens, analytics scripts, or advertising. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process ordinary visitor request information according to GitHub's privacy statement. Merely visiting this site does not connect an account.

Contact and changes

For non-sensitive questions about the integration or this policy, use the repository issue tracker. Do not post private emails, Calendar data, passwords, codes, or tokens there. Material changes to data use or access require an updated policy and, where needed, fresh authorization.
