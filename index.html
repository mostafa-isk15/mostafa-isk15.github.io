Private, owner-operated integration

Email and calendar assistance, with explicit permissions.

Mimu Calendar is a private assistant being configured for its owner's personal productivity. It is not a public mailbox service and does not offer public account registration.

What the assistant is for

After the owner authorizes an account, the integration can help read and summarize email, identify messages that may need attention, and surface travel details and deadlines. Calendar access can support viewing appointments and creating or updating events at the owner's request.

Requested notifications can be delivered through the owner's configured Telegram assistant. Background monitoring depends on the assistant being configured, connected, and running; this website itself does not monitor any inbox.

Separate permissions for mail and Calendar

  • Gmail: read-only message and settings access through gmail.readonly. The mail connection does not request permission to send, delete, archive, or mark messages as read.
  • Google Calendar: event access through calendar.events, which permits reading and editing events. Calendar changes require the owner's instructions or an explicitly approved automation.
  • Other accounts: a work or school mailbox requires its own authorization and must comply with the institution's policies.

Permissions are requested only when the owner connects an account. The owner can revoke them in the provider's account settings.

This is an information site

The pages here describe the integration and its data practices. They do not contain email messages, private Calendar entries, login credentials, or OAuth tokens. They are not an online interface for accessing the assistant.

Read the privacy policy for information about local storage, online AI processing, notifications, retention, and revoking access.
